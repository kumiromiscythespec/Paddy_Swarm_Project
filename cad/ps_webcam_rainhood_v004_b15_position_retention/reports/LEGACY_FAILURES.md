# LEGACY FAILURES AND HOLDS

## Printed PETG pipe clamp

```text
PIPE_OD:
26.2 mm

PIPE_FIT_26P4:
PASS_DIMENSIONAL_REFERENCE

PRINTED_PIPE_CLAMP:
REJECTED

FAIL_BOLT_REACH:
TRUE

FAIL_PRINT_ROBUSTNESS:
TRUE

STATIC_LOAD_TEST:
NOT_TESTED

COMMERCIAL_METAL_CLAMP:
HOLD_SELECTION
```

v004 does not resurrect the rejected printed clamp and does not assume a commercial clamp product or adapter geometry.

## FDM lesson carried forward

Avoid tall thin tabs, thin guide roots, layer-peel-prone ears, and sharp stress risers. v004 uses low continuous guides and outward root feet, but their physical PETG strength remains pending.
