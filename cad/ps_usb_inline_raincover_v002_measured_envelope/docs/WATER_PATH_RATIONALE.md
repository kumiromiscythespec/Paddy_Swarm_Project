# WATER PATH RATIONALE

This cover reduces splash exposure through geometry; it is not a compression seal and is not waterproof.

```text
outside water
    ↓
downward Ø4.4 mm cable entrance
    ↓
10 mm drip vestibule / 5 mm vertical offset
    ↓
terminal baffle and drip interception
    ↓
laterally offset weep
    ↓
Ø2.5 mm gravity drain
```

The entrance is below the connector chamber, changes direction before the chamber, and is screened by the baffle. The floor rises 1.781 mm at center and drains independently to both ends. The drain and weep are laterally offset, so neither cable port nor drain provides a straight line-of-sight splash path to the 95.1 × 18.7 × 10.8 mm connector envelope.

The upper shell retains the 3° roof, 6 mm umbrella skirt, and no roof penetration. The seam retains the 2 mm step, 3 mm radial overlap, and 0.30 mm clearance. The new central external closure station improves long-seam engagement without penetrating the chamber.

Water behavior remains physical-validation pending. The labyrinth coupon must be tested at level, +5°, and -5° with colored water and a dry tissue witness. Mud, grass, insects, and debris may block a 2.5 mm drain; inspect and clear both exits before service. No smaller drain diameter is authorized.

Because camera and extension ends now share the same 4.4 mm geometry, `labyrinth_drain_coupon_v002` is one representative end module. Test it sequentially with each actual cable; this avoids duplicating an identical solid without collapsing the two physical result records.
