# Hydraulic Architecture

Three lowest-stage drops land in the shallow terminal tray. Its 3.5% directional
floor drains to a top-vented Ø25 clear downcomer. The lower elbow discharges
horizontally into a removable 112 × 32 × 30 mm open plenum. A 100 × 9 mm slot
reduces point-jet velocity and drives an upward displacement flow.

The opposite 112 mm weir fixes normal level at Z82. Downstream is an open gutter,
which is an atmospheric air break and cannot act as a siphon. A separate visible
30 mm overflow begins at Z94 and remains independent of the normal outlet.

The normal and drain pads are blank. Final bulkhead geometry is
`DIMENSION_PENDING`. The base configuration has no baffle; the interface is
reserved as `INTERNAL_BAFFLE_RESERVED` and is activated only by dye evidence.

Four towers retain four independent nominal 25 mm hoses all the way to the
central trough. They are never merged upstream. No pipe penetrates the tank floor,
and the Ø100 dry core is not a hydraulic route.
