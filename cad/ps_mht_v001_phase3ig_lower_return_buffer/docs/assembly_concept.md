# Assembly Concept

The lower buffer sits on a metal shelf between adjacent 0° and 90° radial legs.
Its long axis follows the 45° leg gap. Printed cradle D07 only locates and resists
slip; it is not a load member. The lowest module begins above the terminal tray.
The tray surrounds, but does not seal or center on, the dry mast opening.

Assembly order:

1. Install four metal radial legs and the metal shelf/crossbars.
2. Place the D07-qualified cradle and tank without loading the printed cradle vertically.
3. Insert the removable diffuser with its asymmetric key toward the wide slot.
4. Insert the downcomer from above; keep its top vent open.
5. Fit the terminal tray under the lowest stage and align its three landing pads at 60°/180°/300°.
6. Verify the 2 mm Z separation to plant hardware and free upward removal.
7. After fitting selection, connect the open gutter to one independent return hose.

Removal reverses this order. The tank exits upward from the shelf and then outward
along the 45° leg gap after the tray/downcomer are removed.
