# Bambu Studio Review Checklist

Printer: Bambu Lab A1. Material: white PETG. Review each plate independently.

- Confirm overall size is within 245 × 245 × 240 mm.
- Confirm 3 mm critical walls and 4 mm D01 floor survive line-width rounding.
- Place open faces upward; do not generate support inside a wet cavity.
- Inspect every layer around R18 corners, the 100 × 9 mm slot, and both overflow crests.
- Confirm no unsupported internal roof. D02 is open and removable; D03/D04 are open gutters.
- Inspect bridge preview at the downcomer elbow and tray outlet; reject sag that reduces Ø25 clear bore.
- Confirm seam placement is outside wet corner radii and overflow crest edges.
- Confirm flow calibration, dried PETG, and first-layer adhesion before D01.
- D05 holes are coupon/template features only; full tank pads remain blank.

Approval applies only to D01–D07. Full tank, full tray, downcomer assembly, full
integration, four-tower layout, and Phase 3I-F full stage remain `DO_NOT_PRINT`.
