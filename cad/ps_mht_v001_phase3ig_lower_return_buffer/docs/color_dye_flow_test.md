# Color-Dye Flow Test

Status: `COLOR_DYE_TEST_PENDING`. Use water and food-safe water-soluble dye. A
measured transparent container may be used; D08 remains a variable reference
until that container is measured. Record video, flow rate, water level, time, and
ambient conditions for every test.

## Test 1 — normal bottom inflow

Run the intended normal flow. Confirm discharge across the full 100 mm slot, no
single jet, no excessive sediment lift, replacement flow reaches the opposite
upper side, and no corner remains visibly uncolored for an extended interval.

## Test 2 — stepped flow

Step from low to nominal to high flow. Record residence/dead zones, splash,
whether the 112 mm weir flows evenly, and whether level stabilizes near Z82.

## Test 3 — 50% normal-outlet blockage

Reduce the normal outlet area by 50%. Record level rise, continued gutter flow,
tower backpressure, and any intermittent or sustained siphon signature.

## Test 4 — full normal-outlet blockage

Block the normal outlet. The independent overflow must start near Z94, leave
clear margin to the Z105 rim, and discharge visibly away from electrics/walkways.

## Test 5 — pump stop and restart

Stop the pump from steady state. Confirm water below the normal crest stays in
the primary tank, only tower-retained water returns through the gutter, and restart
does not air-lock the downcomer or diffuser.

## Test 6 — cleanout

After the D05 coupon and fitting selection, drain sediment without tilting the
tank. Record retained puddles and inspect the reinforced pad for cracks/leaks.

Allowed results: `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_BAFFLE_RECOMMENDED`, `FAIL_SHORT_CIRCUIT`,
`FAIL_DEAD_ZONE`, `FAIL_SPLASH`, `FAIL_BACKPRESSURE`, `FAIL_NORMAL_OVERFLOW`,
`FAIL_EMERGENCY_OVERFLOW`, `FAIL_CLEANOUT`, or `HOLD`.

Add a baffle only when the opposite corner replaces markedly slower, inlet-to-weir
short-circuiting is visible, a large active-volume fraction stagnates, or the
bottom layer does not refresh at low flow. A baffle is not added merely to tune a
simulation.
