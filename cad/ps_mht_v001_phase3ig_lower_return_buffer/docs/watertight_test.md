# Watertight Test

Print D01 in white PETG after slicer review. Weigh it dry, fill to 20 mm, place on
dry absorbent paper, and inspect at 15 min, 1 h, 6 h, and 24 h. Record layer-line
weeping, corner leakage, mass loss corrected for evaporation, and deformation.

Pass requires no visible wet mark, no bead formation, no crack, and no measurable
loss beyond the control vessel. If D01 fails, hold D02–D07 and every full model;
correct material drying, flow calibration, extrusion width, temperature, or wall
strategy before changing structural thickness.

After D01 passes, repeat a 24 h hold on D03 and D04. The normal gutter is expected
to be atmospheric; seal only the coupon fixture, never the production air break.
