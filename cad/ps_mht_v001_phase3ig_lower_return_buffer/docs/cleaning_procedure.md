# Cleaning Procedure

1. Stop and isolate the pump; allow tower return flow to cease.
2. Remove the terminal tray and lift the vented downcomer upward.
3. Lift the asymmetric diffuser from its wide ledges and brush the 100 × 9 mm slot.
4. Flush the open plenum; there are no fine holes or hidden filter media.
5. After a selected drain fitting exists, open the side cleanout and sweep sediment toward it.
6. Inspect the Z4 floor, R18 corners, blank outlet pads, and emergency lip.
7. Reinstall diffuser in its keyed orientation, then downcomer and tray.

Do not drill a full tank from the D05 drawing alone. A purchased fitting must be
measured and the corresponding drill coupon must pass first.
