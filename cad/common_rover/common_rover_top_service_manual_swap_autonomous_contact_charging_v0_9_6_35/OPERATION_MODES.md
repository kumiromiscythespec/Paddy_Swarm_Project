# Operation modes

- MODE A — NORMAL_LOW_MEDIUM_LOAD_BATTERY_INSTALLED_AUTONOMOUS_CONTACT_CHARGING: battery remains installed; autonomous contact charging; no routine manual swap.
- MODE B — HIGH_LOAD_BUSY_SEASON_AUTONOMOUS_CHARGING_PRIMARY_MANUAL_TOP_SWAP_SUPPLEMENTAL: autonomous charging remains primary; manual top swap may supplement uptime.
- MODE C — SERVICE_FAILURE_STOP_ISOLATE_MOVE_CBOX_OPEN_TOP_MANUAL_REPLACE: rover stopped, motor power isolated, CBOX moved and locked in service position, top opened, battery replaced manually.
- MODE D — FUTURE_AUTOMATIC_SWAP_NOT_CURRENT_BASELINE: rear-slide/robotic swap may be revisited only as a future phase.
