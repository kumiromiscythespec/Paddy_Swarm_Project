The compact 80 mm plan retains 48/46 mm straight gasket runs and all four exact source corners. Four equally placed corner screws give a 68 mm screw span with an 8 mm solid lid; additional screws would not add a justified test variable. This does not qualify stiffness or compression uniformity of the larger eight-screw full BBOX.

The nominal cord centerline perimeter is 219.1018 mm. Nominal fitted cut target: 219.10 mm; an initial 222 mm piece may be trimmed carefully to a relaxed butt fit. Do not stretch or buckle the cord. The joining method remains PHYSICAL_OPERATOR_METHOD / PENDING. Place a joint on a straight run, not at a corner. No CAD notch is made through the seal.

Assembly STEP includes an UNCOMPRESSED Ø1.8 cord reference. Its nominal lid overlap equals the calculated elastic compression allowance; it is not an impossible rigid-part interference or a simulated deformed seal. The rigid body/lid common volume is zero. A separate closed-boundary surrogate is used only to verify that the intended perimeter separates the witness cavity from exterior air; it cannot prove water sealing.

M4 holes are outside the pressure boundary. External pressure-boundary penetration count is zero; the required exterior fastener holes are not vents or holes into the dry cavity.
