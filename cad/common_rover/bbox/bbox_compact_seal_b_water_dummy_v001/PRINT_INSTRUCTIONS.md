# Bambu A1 / PETG

First print the G065 body and common lid. Print G055 later for comparison. Do not print full BBOX artifacts in this phase. No TPU is needed.

- Body: floor down, open side up; 80 × 80 × 29.395 mm. First-layer floor contact 3,720 mm².
- Lid: flat plate, intended seal face UP to keep it off the first layer; invert the lid for assembly. 80 × 80 × 8 mm. First-layer area about 6,281.445 mm². Lid support OFF.
- Both fit comfortably within 256 × 256 × 256 mm.

The inherited tower section begins at Z20.5 and projects up to 9/10 mm beyond the core X/Y walls. The inherited flange projects 4/5 mm. Therefore this design does NOT assert support OFF for the body. Review slicer layers and use external manual support under tower/flange undersides as needed. Never place support in the gasket groove, on the inner seal land or inside the witness cavity. Remove supports without cutting or distorting the sealing plane.

Use established project PETG settings. Slicer has not been run here. Inspect continuous seal walls, groove dimensions, all four corners, layer bonds and flatness. A warped lid/rim or a scar on the seal is a print rejection; do not force it closed. Do not treat CAD watertightness as printed-material waterproofness.

Keep G055 and G065 separated and externally labeled after printing. Their only shape difference is 0.10 mm groove depth. The common lid may be shared, but record lid/gasket identities and whether they were reused during each comparison.
