# Assembly, unpowered and inert only

1. Verify body identity: G065 PRIMARY or G055 comparison. Use the common 8 mm lid.
2. Inspect the groove bottom, four corners, flat rim, hard-stop tops and Ø4.5 holes. Do not ream or abrade the sealing groove to force a fit.
3. Use existing Ø1.8 mm rubber cord. Nominal fitted perimeter is 219.10 mm. If starting from 222 mm rough stock, trim to a relaxed fit; do not stretch or buckle it.
4. Put the butt joint on a straight side, away from corners. Joint method remains operator-defined/pending; record it. No permanent joint is modeled or authorized in CAD.
5. Insert completely dry folded paper/tissue on the clear floor, below and away from the seal. Reference size is 45 × 40 mm.
6. Use four M4 through-bolts with metal washers and nuts. Local printed grip is 8.895 + 8 = 16.895 mm, before hardware. Select actual bolt length from measured washer/nut stack and engagement; no unmeasured hardware length is promoted to authority.
7. Tighten progressively in a diagonal sequence to the hard stops. No destructive tightening or PETG tapped threads. Stop for whitening, cracking, cord displacement or a visible lid seating problem.
8. Observe the physical test plan. Completely wipe exterior water dry BEFORE opening.

The only four drilled-through CAD holes are external M4 mounting holes outside the closed gasket boundary. There is no chimney, gland, cable, vent, drain or cavity penetration.
