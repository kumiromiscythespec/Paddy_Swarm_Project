## 24-hour creep check

1. Mark all four M5 positions.
2. Hold the no-load assembly for 24 hours.
3. Remeasure the four plate gaps.
4. Check nut position changes.
5. Confirm full washer seating.
6. Check aluminum indentation.
7. Check PETG whitening and local indentation.
8. Check diagonal-push rocking.
9. Check the route with thread or a loose, untensioned belt.
10. Finish without applying power.

Acceptance: gap range ≤0.2 mm; no new aluminum indentation, PETG whitening/indentation, washer migration, nut loosening, or rocking. `REQUIRED_BEFORE_BELT_TENSION`.
