# Common Rover Short-M3 Local Bridge Cap Lock v0.9.6.10

Classification: `SHORT_M3_LOCAL_BRIDGE_CAP_LOCK_CORRECTION`  
Release: `PHYSICAL COUPON PREPARATION / NOT FOR POWERED OR FIELD USE`  

Short M3 clamp load flows from front head/washer through the54×5.5 wide cap and broad bridge into the accessible rear washer/nut sandwich. The bridge distributes axial bearing load into nearby hub material through short filleted roots. It is not a thin cantilever and receives no drivetrain torque credit.
