# Common Rover Positive Hub-Cap Lock v0.9.6.8

Classification: `DRIVE_HUB_POSITIVE_LOCK_PHYSICAL_UPDATE`  
Release: `DRY PHYSICAL COUPON PREPARATION / NOT FOR POWERED OR FIELD USE`  

After coupon PASS and full12T print, perform50 hand revolutions, then20 forward+20 reverse with crawler. Stop on screw loosen, nut spin, cap lift/crack, shoe movement, collar/M4 shift, spacer rub, crawler contact, shaft-end contact, belt tooth climb or bearing heat. Normal cap service: remove M3 screws, lift cap by hand, service shoes/M4/collar; no hammer.
