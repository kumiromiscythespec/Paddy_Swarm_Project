# Common Rover Y3 Reaction Shoe v0.9.6.19

Classification: `DRIVE_Y3_REMOVABLE_REACTION_SHOE`.

1. Remove cap. 2. Insert/remove each shoe through the open service path. 3. Seat both shoes at their positive stops. 4. Install cap. 5. Slide Dual-L to LOCK. 6. Install stop key. Shoe service with cap present is prohibited by the parent sequence.
