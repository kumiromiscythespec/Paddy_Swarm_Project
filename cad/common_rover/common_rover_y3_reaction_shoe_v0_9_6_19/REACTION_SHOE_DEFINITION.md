# Common Rover Y3 Reaction Shoe v0.9.6.19

Classification: `DRIVE_Y3_REMOVABLE_REACTION_SHOE`.

`Y3 RECEIVER` is the PETG pocket/island in the 12T main body. `Y3 REACTION SHOE` is the removable PETG insert transferring metal-collar/headed-M4 reaction into broad receiver shoulders. Historical “reaction yoke” appears only in source trace. Quantity is two per sprocket.
