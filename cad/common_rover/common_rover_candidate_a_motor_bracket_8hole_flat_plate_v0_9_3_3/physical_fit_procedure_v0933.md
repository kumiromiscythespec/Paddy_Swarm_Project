# Physical fit procedure v0.9.3.3

Scope: single-part, no-load, no-power trial fit. `FASTENER_TORQUE = MEASUREMENT_HOLD`; hand-tight only without deforming PETG.

1. Print only one PETG T6 plate.
2. Confirm all eight holes are fully open.
3. Pass an M3 screw through each Ø3.4 hole.
4. Pass an M5 screw through each Ø5.7 hole.
5. Trial-position the M5 bolts and T-nuts on the 2040 frame.
6. Install the same M5 nut/spacer construction at all four positions.
7. Equalize all four spacer heights.
8. Place the plate on the M5 spacers.
9. Check plate rocking and tilt.
10. Place the metal motor bracket on the plate.
11. Align the bracket M3 holes with the plate M3 holes.
12. Insert the M3 bolts.
13. Install M3 nuts below the plate.
14. Confirm M3 nuts and screw tips do not contact the 2040 frame.
15. Confirm M5 heads and washers do not interfere with the bracket.
16. Confirm equal four-point plate support.
17. Confirm full-face seating of the metal bracket.
18. Move the assembly lightly by hand and check for large play.
19. Measure the motor-shaft height.
20. Finish without applying power.

Do not tension a belt, rotate under power, apply torque load, or deploy.
