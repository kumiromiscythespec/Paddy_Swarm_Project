# Adapter print and fit procedure v0.9.3.2

`NO POWER / NO ROTATION / NO BELT TENSION / NO TORQUE LOAD`

1. Print the M4 calibration coupon.
2. Select the best M4 pilot using the actual screw.
3. Print one C360 or C370 adapter.
4. Pass M5x16 through each 5.7 mm hole.
5. Confirm the captive washer seats flat.
6. Hand-tighten M5 screws into compatible 2040 T-nuts.
7. Confirm no screw tip bottoms in the slot.
8. Place the metal bracket on the adapter.
9. Confirm the 40.2 x 40.0 mm footprint is fully supported.
10. Confirm all four 24 x 30 mm bracket holes align.
11. Hand-tighten the M4 screws.
12. Inspect PETG for cracks, whitening, or bulging.
13. Confirm M5 heads and washers do not touch the bracket.
14. Measure the motor-shaft position.
15. Measure the Z increase caused by the adapter.
16. Decide whether a second identical adapter may be printed.
17. Finish without energizing or rotating the motor.
