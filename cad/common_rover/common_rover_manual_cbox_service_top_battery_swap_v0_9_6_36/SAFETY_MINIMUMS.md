# Safety minimums

`SELF_SERVICE ≠ SAFETY_RELAXATION`. The initial freeware baseline retains:

- `FUSE`
- `POWER_ISOLATION`
- `POLARITY_PROTECTION`
- `STRAIN_RELIEF`
- `CABLE_ENTANGLEMENT_PREVENTION`
- `WATERPROOF_BOUNDARY`
- `EXPOSED_LIVE_CONDUCTOR_PREVENTION`

Motor and charging power must be isolated before battery service. The CBOX and cable must be retained away from crawler, PTO, motor, shaft, frame pinch, water, and exposed conductor hazards before restart.
