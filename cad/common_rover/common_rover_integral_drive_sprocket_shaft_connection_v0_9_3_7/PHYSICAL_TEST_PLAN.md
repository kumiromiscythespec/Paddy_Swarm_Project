# PHYSICAL_TEST_PLAN

Prerequisites: no power, no load, no maximum tension, no mud/water, and no irreversible shaft work.

1. Inspect the sprocket exterior.
2. Confirm exactly 12 teeth.
3. Inspect every tooth root for layer or fusion defects.
4. Inspect both H0 clamp halves.
5. Measure the physical nominal 10 mm shaft diameter.
6. Compare the 10.0 / 10.1 / 10.2 mm coupons.
7. Record the selected physical bore candidate; do not treat 10.1 mm as selected without this result.
8. Fit H0 around the unmodified round shaft without forced insertion.
9. Install the two M5 through-bolts, metal washers, and metal nuts finger-tight.
10. Tighten left/right evenly in stages.
11. Add a witness mark across shaft and H0 hub.
12. Add a witness mark across H0 hub and S0 sprocket.
13. Check initial axial movement.
14. With no crawler, hand rotate forward 10 turns.
15. With no crawler, hand rotate reverse 10 turns.
16. Inspect both witness marks for movement.
17. Install the crawler at minimum tension only.
18. Hand rotate forward one revolution.
19. Hand rotate reverse one revolution.
20. Hand rotate forward 10 revolutions.
21. Hand rotate reverse 10 revolutions.
22. Inspect tooth entry into every link receiver.
23. Inspect shaft-to-hub relative rotation.
24. Inspect hub-to-sprocket relative rotation.
25. Inspect PETG whitening and cracking.
26. Inspect permanent split-gap deformation.
27. Inspect bearing migration and verify no shield/outer-race contact.
28. Recheck axial movement.

## H0 candidate acceptance observations

- no forced press fit
- clamping holds only after bolt tightening
- no forward/reverse witness-mark motion
- no axial motion
- no PETG whitening or crack
- no center shift
- no bearing-shield contact
- no fastener-to-link contact

Even a compliant H0 result does not approve powered rotation, torque load, or field use.
