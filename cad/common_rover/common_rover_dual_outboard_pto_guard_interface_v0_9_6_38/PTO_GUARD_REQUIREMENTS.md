# Common Rover dual outboard PTO guard interface v0.9.6.38

The PTO guard is mandatory. It covers TOP, FRONT and OUTBOARD faces and is removable for inspection and tool access. BOTTOM and LOWER_REAR remain open for mud discharge, drainage and washout; this is not a sealed box.

Guard clearance 3 mm is a packaging candidate and 5 mm is retained as sensitivity. Final clearance, material, fastener, stiffness, debris test and rotating proof remain HOLD. The STEP guard is a reference envelope, not production guard CAD.
