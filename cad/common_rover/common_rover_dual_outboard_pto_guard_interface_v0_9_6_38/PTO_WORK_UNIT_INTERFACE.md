# Common Rover dual outboard PTO guard interface v0.9.6.38

The PTO interface transmits rotational torque only. A dedicated hitch and guide frame carries work-unit weight, side reaction, bending, hitch force and impact. Box walls are not a structural load path.

Future work units may use LEFT_ONLY, RIGHT_ONLY or DUAL operation. Work-unit-side ratio, pulley and belt remain flexible and product-selection HOLD.
