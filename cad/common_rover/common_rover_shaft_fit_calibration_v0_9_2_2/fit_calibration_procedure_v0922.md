# Physical fit calibration procedure v0.9.2.2

`NO_LOAD_ONLY` — `PHYSICAL_TEST_NOT_PERFORMED`

1. Do not use power, motor or belt.
2. Use the same 10 mm shaft.
3. Clean the shaft surface.
4. Measure the shaft at three axial positions at 0° and 90°.
5. Test coupons without modifying them.
6. Record the state before burr removal.
7. Try P samples from the lowest number.
8. Lightly close the split clamp until its hard-stop lands touch.
9. Check lateral play.
10. Inspect the shaft surface for damage.
11. Confirm hand disassembly.
12. Try S samples from the lowest number.
13. Check straight sliding without twisting.
14. Move continuously over 20–30 mm.
15. Place at 0, 5 and 10 mm.
16. Check lateral play.
17. Check self-drop behavior.
18. Select at most one compliant sample per family.
19. If none complies, select none.
20. Record measurements and photo IDs.
21. Finish without applying power.

## Prohibited

Hammering, plier press-fit, drill rotation, motor rotation, belt tension, shaft
cutting, hole enlargement, omitting pre-deburr observations, sanding before the
initial record, lubrication that changes the result, machining and powered test.

## Selection

Positioning: no visible play; stop reached; no damage; hand removable; minimum
closing force among compliant candidates. Sliding: straight no-twist movement,
0/5/10 mm placement, no visible play, no local seizure, no damage, no rapid
self-drop; choose the smallest bore among compliant candidates. Both selected
diameters remain `HOLD` until this procedure is physically completed.
