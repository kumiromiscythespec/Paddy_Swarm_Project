# Phase 3I-D print and assembly instructions

The Phase 3I-D full stage and D04D/D05D diagnostics are not printable approvals. Load each diagnostic as one object in a new Bambu Studio project and do not print it.

Review the upper coupon first in its registered orientation with support OFF. If it passes, review the lower full-height coupon the same way. The lower coupon includes a wide, dry-test-only annular base and two dry alignment guides with 0.5 mm side clearance to the upper coupon. These test fixtures are not part of the production full model and do not close the central opening or touch hydraulic faces.

After both slicer reviews pass, print and assemble the coupons at 170 mm pitch. Do not use a shim unless deliberately evaluating the specified ±2 mm offsets. Confirm that the upper drip edge is at global Z176, the lower receiver top is at Z170, and neither printed part bridges the 6 mm air gap.
