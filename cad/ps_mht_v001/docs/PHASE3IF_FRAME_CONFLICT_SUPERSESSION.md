# Frame clocking conflict supersession

The Phase 3I-E 30-degree hydraulic layout cleared the pots, retention lugs and corrected body, but rotating a local −X fixing feature moved the five attachment directions to 180, 210, 240, 270 and 300 degrees. One fixed rear post therefore could not receive every stage interface.

Phase 3I-F records that interface as `SUPERSEDED_BY_NON_ROTATING_DRY_CORE_FRAME`. The rear post remains fixed at reference centre (−150, 0), while lower and upper metal links connect it to the central non-rotating mast. No legacy artifact is deleted or overwritten.
