# Phase 3I-C keeper V2 print and test

Print one keeper alone, flat, in PETG Basic with a 0.4 mm nozzle. Use no support and normally no brim.

Check that it lies flat on the real pot flange, leaves the plant center open, stays inside the flange edge, accepts the 5 mm cord from above through both notches, resists rotation under light cord tension, spreads load without bending the flange, and removes without tools after untying.

Complete 20 install/remove cycles and record whitening, cracking, notch damage, cord damage, and flange damage. Combine it with the Phase 3I-B cradle coupon for the 500 g outward-load test. Physical retention remains pending until this test is completed.
