# Phase 3I-F non-rotating dry-core frame

Phase 3I-F supersedes the stage-local −X rear fixing concept. Five growing stages remain at absolute rotations 0, 30, 60, 90 and 120 degrees while a non-rotating metal mast passes through the existing dry Ø100 central opening. No stage is bolted individually to the mast and no wet wall is penetrated.

The reference mast is nominal 20 × 20 mm 2020 aluminium extrusion. This is a geometric reference, not a selected commercial dimension. Actual width in both directions, corner radii, twist, straightness and total length must be measured before a production puck or cap is generated.

The installed reference puck and cap use an outer diameter of 98.0 mm and remain inside the 49.0 mm dry-core radius authority. The 98.5 and 99.0 mm parts exist only as temporary fit coupons. The selected puck diameter and mast clearance remain `None` until physical calibration.

Vertical plant and module weight travels through the cradles, module shells, dry stacking seats, lowest stage and base. The mast restrains lateral motion, tilt and separation only. Upper and lower links to the rear post are metal-plate references with M5/T-nut fastening; printed parts are not represented as primary bending members.
