# Phase 3I-A Water and Overflow Test Plan

CAD Boolean volumes, after subtracting all printed intrusions and the exact R3 floor/wall fillet, are approximately 0.346463 L at 18 mm, 0.365767 L at 19 mm, and 0.385071 L at 20 mm. The 18 mm candidate is below the 0.35 L acceptance floor. The selected CAD candidate is 20 mm; it is not a physical result.

After the lower coupon passes dry inspection, add clear water in measured increments. Record first leakage, actual retained volume at the crest, overflow-start volume, remaining pockets after drainage, and whether a brush reaches every wet surface.

Test steady 0.5 L/min and 1.0 L/min inflow, followed by a short 2.0 L/min transient. Record crest head, direct spill continuity, root/debris sensitivity, air entrainment, and any rise toward the structural wall top. The 25 mm-wide, minimum 8 mm open throat provides 200 mm² of geometric opening, but no hydraulic capacity is claimed without this physical test. No CFD result is implied.
