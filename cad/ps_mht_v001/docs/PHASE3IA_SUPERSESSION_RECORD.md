# Phase 3I-A Supersession Record

The Phase 3H-A/B arc and full-ring results remain preserved evidence. C050 passed the reported arc test and the 360-degree mechanical test, including 20 full-ring cycles without whitening, cracking, or abnormal wear. Its 0.50 mm/side value is retained only as a dry stacking-guide reference.

Compression ring V1 was printed once and failed lateral centering. Its status is `SUPERSEDED_BY_PHASE_3IA`. Compression ring V2 is not implemented and a second V1 ring is not required.

The following production architecture is superseded: thin shell plus wet horizontal joint, upper/lower compression rings, temporary membrane or gasket, and mandatory interstage watertightness. Existing Phase 3H files and physical records are not deleted, overwritten, or reclassified as failed geometry.

Phase 3I-A uses stage-local water retention, an open weir to the next stage, dry segmented positioning guides, and the rear frame as the primary lateral support.
