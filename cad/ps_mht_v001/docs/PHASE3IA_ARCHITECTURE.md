# Phase 3I-A Integrated Wet-Base Architecture

Phase 3I-A replaces the thin-shell, horizontal wet joint, compression-ring, and temporary-membrane production concept with one vertically printed 170 mm growing stage. The stage combines a 360-degree structural body, three open gravity cradles, an open annular sump, three visible root/wick routes, an open rear cascade, an open overflow weir, dry stacking guides, lower-weighted ribs, and a rear-frame datum.

Each stage retains its own approximately 0.4 L water inventory. Overflow falls openly to the next stage; the stacking plane is not a wet seal. The previously measured C050 0.50 mm/side fit remains a dry-guide reference only.

The new coordinate authority uses +Z up, +X front, +Y left, and -X rear service. The legacy frame reference used +Y as rear, so its reference post is rotated 90 degrees about Z for Phase 3I-A; no legacy source is changed.

The body is not a standalone thin cylinder. The 4 mm floor, 4.8 mm lower wall, port frames, 8 mm cradle gussets, three 3.6 mm internal ribs, rear channel walls, 4.5 mm top band, and stacking-guide bases share a continuous floor/wall load path. The central 100 mm opening remains open.

The full model is a slicer-review artifact only. Bambu Studio remains authoritative for support placement, islands, layer-area changes, time, and filament estimates. No print success, water tightness, overflow performance, or production readiness is claimed by CAD generation.
