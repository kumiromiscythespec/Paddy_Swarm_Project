# Phase 3H-B Print and Assembly Instructions

## Plate sequence

1. Print `plate_01_full_ring_lower_c050_phase3hb.stl` alone and complete its dimensional and visual inspection.
2. Only after Plate 01 inspection, print `plate_02_full_ring_upper_c050_phase3hb.stl` alone and complete its inspection.
3. Assemble and physically validate the full-ring C050 pair.
4. Keep `plate_03_compression_ring_HOLD_phase3hb.stl` on hold until every release criterion in the mechanical test plan passes.

## Orientation

The lower ring is printed in assembly Z orientation: its bottom is on the build plate and the receiver opening and hard-stop face point upward.

The upper ring is rotated 180 degrees from assembly posture: the body top is on the build plate and the thin skirt and hard-stop face point upward. The mating surface is not placed on the build plate.

Both full rings are approximately 202 mm in outer envelope and fit the 256 x 256 mm A1 XY range. Print each alone.

## Print restrictions

Do not print sideways or at an angle. Do not use automatic support or support on a mating face. Do not split or sectorize either ring, put upper and lower on one plate, add small auxiliary feet, or reuse arc-coupon feet. No CAD brim is present. If bed adhesion requires it, an outer slicer brim of 5–8 mm may be used without touching a mating surface.

## Assembly restrictions

Remove only ordinary strings or loose contamination without changing the nominal mating geometry. Do not force assembly with tools, heat, lubricant, sanding, filing, or clamping. If either ring is measurably oval or warped, stop and classify the print before making any clearance decision.
