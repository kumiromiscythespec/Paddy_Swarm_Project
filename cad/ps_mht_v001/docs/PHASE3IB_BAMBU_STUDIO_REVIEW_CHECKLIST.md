# Phase 3I-B Bambu Studio review checklist

Review the full STL and the cradle-sector STL before printing anything. With supports off, inspect detached islands, unanchored bridge lines, wall interruption, thin independent towers, every three-pad buttress, both lugs, wick rails, rear chute, and stacking guides. Then display automatic supports and record whether support is generated at the cradle, inside the sump, at lugs, or at guides.

Record `floating_region_warning`, all `support_off` fields, all `automatic_support` fields, and `support_dependency` as `NONE` or `PRESENT`. CAD currently records every one as `PENDING`; it does not claim a Bambu Studio pass.
