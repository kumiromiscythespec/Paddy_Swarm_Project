# Phase 3I-B real sump test plan

Use the 40 mm full annular coupon on a level surface with dry paper beneath the Ø100 opening. Add clean water in 0.10 L increments and record the actual overflow-start and retained volumes. Inspect the central opening, outer wall, wick entries, and rear chute during fill and after 12 hours.

Then apply 0.5 L/min, 1.0 L/min, and a temporary 2.0 L/min flow. Record direct overflow, unintended wall overtopping, leakage, trapped pockets, and brush access. CFD or CAD throat area is not a flow-pass authority.

The 40 mm height is the minimum candidate in the requested 35–45 mm range that includes the Z31 dam top, Z26 crest, Z28 wick entry, rear chute lower portion, and all water-contacting buttress roots.
