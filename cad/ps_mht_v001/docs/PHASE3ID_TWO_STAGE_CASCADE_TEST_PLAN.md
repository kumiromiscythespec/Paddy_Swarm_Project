# Phase 3I-D two-stage cascade test plan

Use clean water only. Assemble the full-scale upper drop-chute coupon at Z+170 above the lower receiver/channel coupon. Verify the 6 mm air gap before applying water.

Nominal tests are 0.5 L/min for 5 minutes, 1.0 L/min for 5 minutes and 2.0 L/min for 1 minute. Repeat 0.5 and 1.0 L/min at tangential ±2 mm, radial ±2 mm and rotation ±2°. The planar CAD envelope also covers same-sign tangential ±2 mm plus rotation ±2°.

For every run inspect total receiver capture, dry paper under the central opening, exterior wall runoff, receiver splash, channel splash, arrival at the lower sump, stagnant pools, drainage within 30 seconds, brush access, the dry air gap and residual capillary bridges.

If measurement resolution is adequate, record `capture_ratio_percent = lower_collected_volume / upper_supplied_volume × 100`. Candidate thresholds are ≥99% at 0.5 and 1.0 L/min and ≥98% at 2.0 L/min, with no visible external or central-hole leakage. Record measuring vessels, scale resolution and uncertainty; do not imply unsupported precision.
