# Phase 3I-A Net-Pot Installation

Use the measured Siawadeky pot: 108 mm flange, 4 mm flange thickness, 68 mm overall height, and 78.6 mm maximum body/rib diameter. The physically selected body passage is 80.5 mm. Internal diameters 77.2, 75.8, and 69.6 mm are reference-only and never define the external envelope.

Align the pot with the 27-degree outward axis and lower it onto the open U cradle. The lower continuous seat and two side regions support the flange; no snap, gate, cartridge, or top retainer is present. The flange must seat without tools and the body must remain clear of the 80.5 mm passage.

The selected 2.0 mm radial recess gives a conservative installed maximum XY diameter of 237.215 mm. Remove the pot along the same axis. The open 16 mm wick route permits visual access and should allow the pot to be removed without cutting roots; this remains subject to a physical mesh/root trial.
