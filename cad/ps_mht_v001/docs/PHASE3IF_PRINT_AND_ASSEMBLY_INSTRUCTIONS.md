# Phase 3I-F print and assembly instructions

Use the registered flat orientation for puck and cap parts and the registered vertical orientation for the full module. Supports are off. The full module is slicer-review-only and must not be printed.

For a dry five-stage trial, assemble rotations 0, 30, 60, 90 and 120 degrees using the removable 30-degree clocking gauge. Install the bottom puck at the base, insert the measured metal mast without forcing it, fit the removable top cap and apply only light preload through metal M5 hardware. Remove the upper bracket and top cap before lifting stages upward for disassembly.

The rear post links are metal structural references. Do not substitute printed PETG bars as the primary 150 mm span. Do not hang the tower mass from the mast or cap. Hydraulic testing starts only after the dry frame and repeated disassembly tests pass.
