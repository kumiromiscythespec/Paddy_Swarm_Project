# Phase 3I-A Lower 60 mm Coupon Test Plan

Print only after Bambu Studio review. Use clear water only. Do not use nutrient solution, plants, adhesive, sealant, paint, sanding, or surface finishing to rescue a failed part.

The coupon contains the production floor, complete annular sump, 4.8 mm lower wall, actual floor transition, lower portions of all three cradles and port frames, cradle gussets, wick guides, rear channel, open weir, central opening, and production vertical orientation. It intentionally omits the upper general wall, top guide, upper port region, and upper inlet.

Record bed adhesion, rocking, maximum base gap, leakage, measured operating volume, overflow-start volume, overflow flow, residual-water pockets, cradle spaghetti, unsupported-overhang damage, rear-channel brush access, wick-path access, whitening, and cracking. Any leak, inaccessible retained support, blocked open route, or structural print failure stops progression to the full stage.
