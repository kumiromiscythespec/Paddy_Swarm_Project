# Phase 3I-B architecture

Phase 3I-B keeps the 170 mm, one-piece, vertical, three-port stage and corrects three functional boundaries: a real annular sump, floor-connected pot supports, and positive retention.

The sump floor spans radius 50–100 mm at Z0–4. A 360-degree dam spans radius 50–54.8 mm to Z31. The wet-side root has an exact R3 transition; the dry opening remains a clear Ø100 mm. The selected CAD operating surface is Z26, leaving 5 mm to the dam top. The only intended lowest outlet is the 30 mm open rear weir at −X.

Each port uses one 40 mm main pad and two 20 mm side pads. Solid lofted buttresses start in the floor and expand into the three inclined seat pads. There is no independent cradle ring. Two large lugs per port continue from the floor/side-buttress region and use a 5.6 mm-wide, 45-degree-roof teardrop opening for commercial 5 mm vinyl rope.

The optional C keeper is a flat 165-degree annular load spreader, Ø108/Ø90 × 4.5 mm. The rope remains the primary retention device.

CAD topology and dimensional audits are not physical watertightness, overflow-flow, retention, or Bambu Studio approval.
