# Phase 3I-B net-pot retention test plan

Use the one-port sector coupon, an unplanted dry siawadeky pot, commercial 5 mm vinyl rope, and optionally the C keeper. Verify gravity seating first, then route the rope between both large lugs across the outer flange sector without crossing the plant center.

Record manual outward pull, vertical lift, a 500 g outward load for 30 minutes, 20 install/remove cycles, whitening, cracking, rope damage, flange damage, and tool-free removal after untying. The C keeper spreads load but is not required for the cord-only retention check.
