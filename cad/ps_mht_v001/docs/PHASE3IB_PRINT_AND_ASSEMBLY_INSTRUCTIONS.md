# Phase 3I-B print and assembly instructions

1. Review Plate 01 and Plate 03 in Bambu Studio.
2. Confirm that floating regions and support dependency are absent.
3. Print Plate 02 and complete the sump/water/overflow tests.
4. Print Plate 03 and test the real pot, 5 mm rope, and optional keeper.
5. Print Plate 04 flat only if load spreading is desired.
6. Move Plate 01 to print-candidate status only after every preceding review and physical test passes.

All module and coupon STLs remain upright. Sideways printing and segmentation are prohibited. Phase 3I-A STLs remain prohibited.
