# Puck fit and mast measurement

Print only `plate_01_dry_core_puck_fit_coupons_phase3if.stl` first. The plate contains 98.0, 98.5 and 99.0 mm outside-diameter candidates with a nominal 21.0 mm square reference opening. The latter is not a production clearance.

Measure the printed central opening at multiple angles and heights. Measure the selected metal mast in both width directions, its corner radius or chamfer, twist, straightness and length. Record insertion force, lateral play, whitening and removal for 20 cycles. The part must not require spreading or force and must not inhibit stage rotation.

Do not print the held full puck or top cap until both the central-opening fit and real mast dimensions are selected. The 98.5 and 99.0 mm pieces are calibration coupons only; the installed dry-core authority remains at or below radius 49.0 mm unless a later requirement explicitly changes it.
