# Phase 3I-D architecture

Phase 3I-D freezes all 46 Phase 3I-C official artifacts. Phase 3I-C D01 through D03, keeper V2, the complete six-lug retention system, cradle, sump, inner dam, port geometry and 2 mm port recess are reused exactly. Old Phase 3I-C D04, D05 and corrected full remain historical hydraulically incomplete models and are prohibited from printing.

The new authority is `build_integrated_stage_phase3id(...)`. D04D adds the positive open cascade and the existing open wick routes to frozen D03. D05D adds unchanged stacking guides. D05D and the Phase 3I-D corrected full use the same builder and all-true flags.

Each stage includes both ends of the interstage interface: an outgoing rear downchute terminating at local Z6 and an incoming receiver at Z170. Stacked at 170 mm pitch, the upper drip nose is at global Z176 and the lower receiver top at Z170, leaving a nominal 6 mm dry air gap. No geometry crosses local Z0 or Z170.

The incoming route is a rear-outward open U-channel with a full visual and brush path. Its Z28 discharge ramp points into the annular sump. The central Ø100 mm opening remains dry and is prohibited as a water route.
