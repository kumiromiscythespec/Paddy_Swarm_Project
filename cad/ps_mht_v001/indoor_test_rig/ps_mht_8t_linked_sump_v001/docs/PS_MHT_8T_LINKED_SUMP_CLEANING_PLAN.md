# Linked Zone Cleaning Plan

## Routine inspection

Inspect LOW/NORMAL/HIGH marks, sump sediment, roots, media/wick fragments,
strainer restriction, valve guards, hose support, trunk low point, dry safety
trough and emergency receiver. Unexpected trough moisture is an abnormal event.

## Branch cleaning

Close upper supply, allow tower drainage, confirm stable local level, then
close equalization. Withdraw the sump toward the aisle. Remove sediment and
debris with all surfaces visible; do not leave inaccessible bottom cavities.
Flush the branch, bulkhead and valve without sending suspect liquid back to
the shared trunk.

## Trunk and pump well

Use end cleanouts for brush or flushing-water access. Drain at the low point,
inspect the settling bay, remove and clean the intake strainer, then record
flow before/after cleaning. Ensure the pump stays submerged and never lift it
by its electrical cord.

## Disease response

Immediately isolate a suspect tower at both upper supply and equalization
branches. Treat its liquid, sump and service tools as segregated. Clean before
reconnection and verify EC/pH compatibility. Because all active branches share
solution, cleaning does not make disease transmission risk zero; evaluate the
whole zone and replace solution/components when indicated.

## Fresh-water separation

Clean the upper 10 L fresh-water tank as a separate system. Never make a closed
cross-connection between clean water and recirculating nutrient solution.
