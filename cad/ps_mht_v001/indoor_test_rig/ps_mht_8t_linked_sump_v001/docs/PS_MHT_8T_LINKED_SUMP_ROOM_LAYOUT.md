# Room Layout Reference

The ceiling height is confirmed at 2300 mm. `room_length_mm` and
`room_width_mm` remain `MEASUREMENT_PENDING`; a generic six-tatami room is not
used as a fixed envelope.

The four-tower reference uses four active branches of the eight-branch
architecture. A separate eight-position reference shows expansion and four
initially closed branches. Neither STEP establishes room fit.

Layout rules:

- Human aisle width is at least 600 mm, preferably 700-800 mm.
- Local sumps withdraw toward the aisle, with valve handles facing the aisle.
- The equalization trunk follows a protected rear service route.
- No water pipe crosses the walking aisle or remains exposed where stepped on.
- The normally dry emergency trough runs along the wall/rear side.
- Water piping and electrical wiring use separated routes.
- Dry tower frames and fresh-water shelving transfer load to the floor/wall
  structure, not to local sumps or towers.

The fresh-water shelf reference is 1650-1750 mm, nominally 1700 mm. Tank top
elevation is at most 2050 mm, providing at least 250 mm ceiling clearance.
Final plan coordinates wait for room length, width, doors, furniture,
electrical outlets, drain access and egress measurement.
