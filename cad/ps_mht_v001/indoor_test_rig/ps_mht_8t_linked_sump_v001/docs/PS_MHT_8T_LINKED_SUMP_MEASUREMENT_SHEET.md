## Bulkhead fitting

Record nominal size, body OD, required hole diameter, thread length, gasket OD,
gasket thickness, minimum flat width and allowed wall thickness. Final hole
diameter remains unset until all are measured.

## Full-port union ball valve

Record total length, maximum body OD, union OD, handle sweep envelope and hose
connection geometry in open and closed positions.

## Trunk, branch hose and clamps

Record actual ID/OD, material, minimum bend radius, flexibility and usable
clamp range. Record cleaning-cap brush/flush access.

## Pump

Record body envelope, intake position, minimum submergence, cable exit,
discharge nipple OD/ID and measured head-flow points. Do not use the cable as
a handle.

## Room and support

Record room length/width, door/egress, outlet positions, usable rear route,
floor level, tower loaded mass, centre of mass and anchoring structure.
Ceiling height is already confirmed at 2300 mm.
