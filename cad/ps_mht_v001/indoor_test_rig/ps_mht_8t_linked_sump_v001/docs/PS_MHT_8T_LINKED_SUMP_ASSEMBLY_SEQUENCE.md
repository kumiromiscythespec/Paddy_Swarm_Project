# Reference Assembly and Commissioning Sequence

1. Measure room floor, service route, egress and electrical separation.
2. Measure purchased sumps, pump well, bulkheads, valves, hose and pump.
3. Validate the dry support frame for actual tower mass and stability.
4. Place the rear trunk supports, cleanout access and low-point drain.
5. Place the normally dry safety trough and non-circulating receiver.
6. Install the central purchased pump well without using pipe or cable as a
   handle or support.
7. Place each purchased local sump under an unloaded dry tower frame.
8. Independently support bulkheads, valves and flexible transition hose.
9. Connect branches A-H in parallel; keep unused branches closed, drained and
   identified.
10. Install independent HIGH overflow paths and verify they do not reconnect
    to circulation.
11. Install upper manifold, individual valves and always-available bypass.
12. Install the separate fresh-water tank on its independent shelf.
13. Flush, inspect, leak-test and clean all wetted purchased components.
14. Commission four towers, then six, then eight only after the previous flow,
    water-balance, overflow and energy stage passes.

This is a sequence authority, not a dimension authority. Final holes, brackets
and supports wait for physical measurement.
